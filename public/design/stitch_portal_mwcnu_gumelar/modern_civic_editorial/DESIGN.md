## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional yet hyper-contemporary visual language for a modern civic and religious body. Drawing direct influence from high-craft tech platforms like Framer and Vercel alongside Swiss editorial design, the identity replaces outdated bureaucratic ornaments with razor-sharp execution, immense whitespace, and architectural balance. 

The emotional tone is deeply authoritative, progressive, and pristine. It commands institutional respect through discipline, avoiding visual clutter in favor of monumental typographic weight, hairline-precise surfaces, and surgical focal points. The aesthetic aligns with international design award standards: stark contrast, deliberate content density modulation, and fluid micro-surfaces that convey technological maturity while honoring civic responsibility.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly around high visual tension and controlled emphasis:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** The base canvas is pure, uncompromising white (`#FFFFFF`). Surface layers modulate softly to ultra-light cool tones: Surface Low (`#F8FAFC`) for resting card structures and Surface Medium (`#F1F5F9`) for interactive groupings and secondary badges.
- **Institutional Primary (`#064E3B`):** A deep, saturated Emerald Forest. Used strictly as a monolithic focal anchor—primary hero buttons, key interactive callouts, and decisive branding marks. It is never diluted or applied haphazardly across broad backgrounds.
- **Primary Ink (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate Black, driving high-contrast typographic readability without the harsh deadness of pure pitch black. Subdued text levels transition to Slate 600 (`#475569`) and Muted Text (`#94A3B8`).
- **Interactive Accents:** Hover states and micro-interactions utilize crystalline tints, predominantly Pale Mint (`#ECFDF5`) for interactive fills, with Emerald (`#10B981`) reserved strictly for live status indicators and subtle edge highlights.
- **Rules of Balance:** Maintain a 90/8/2 field distribution: 90% white and off-white negative space, 8% crisp slate text hierarchy, and 2% deep emerald focal energy.

## Typography

The typographic strategy strictly bans serifs and dated administrative scripts. The entire system is built upon modern geometric clarity:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set exclusively in Plus Jakarta Sans (weights 700 to 800/Black). Large scale titles utilize an aggressive negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) paired with tight line heights to achieve a commanding, editorial lockup reminiscent of luxury contemporary monographs and high-end software sites.
- **Body:** Rendered in neutral, highly legible weights (400 and 500) of Plus Jakarta Sans. Generous line spacing (`1.6` to `1.65`) guarantees effortless reading over dense public declarations and structural agendas.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Inter serves functional elements, data tables, and badges. Category markers utilize `label-caps` in all uppercase with tracked-out spacing (`0.08em`) to construct sharp contrast against oversized editorial titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a strict 12-column architectural grid on desktop, shifting to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column framework on mobile devices.

- **Negative Space as Luxury:** Content sections are defined by extreme vertical separation. Layout modules must be granted generous breathing space (`space-xl` through double margins) rather than boxed divisions.
- **Layout Modularity:** Max container widths stay fixed at `1280px` for dense institutional records and expand to `1440px` for visual showcase headers. 
- **Component Breathing Room:** Components rely on oversized interior padding. Cards never compress their contents; minimum card interior padding starts at `space-md` on mobile and expands to `space-lg` on desktop to maintain an uncluttered presence.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids layered dropshadows, muddy skeuomorphism, and standard corporate box shadows. Depth is achieved purely through value transitions and razor-thin boundaries:

- **The Floating Surface:** Elevated cards sit on pure `#FFFFFF` over an off-white `#F8FAFC` foundation, or sit on `#F8FAFC` against a stark white background.
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Physical boundaries use ultra-subtle, low-contrast 1px outlines (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`). Borders are crisp, non-distracting, and disappear into the background on higher-tier monitors.
- **Ambient Shadow Craft:** In interactive states (e.g., active dropdowns, modal windows, floating sticky navigation bars), shadows are ultra-diffuse and tinted:
  - Base resting: No shadow; hairline outline only.
  - Floating layer: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  - Modal overlay: `0 30px 60px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Glass Refinement:** Navigation elements fixed to the viewport utilize a frosted crystal blur: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, backed by a hairline baseline border.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by continuous, ultra-smooth curvature:

- **Cards and Major Containers:** Standard containers rely heavily on `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem / 24px) continuous radii, creating modern, pillowed structural blocks that soften the impact of high-contrast black and emerald tones.
- **Buttons and Chips:** Interactive buttons and filter chips feature smooth rounded geometry or full stadium pills (`rounded-full`) for status indicators and micro-badges.
- **Inner Nested Elements:** When nesting interactive elements or images inside a card, the inner corner radius must follow the concentric formula ($R_{inner} = R_{outer} - \text{padding}$) to ensure pristine geometric continuity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald background (`#064E3B`), crisp pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), bold weight (`fontWeight: 700`), `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`. Hover transitions smoothly to a darkened emerald (`#022C22`) with a subtle `-translate-y-0.5` lift. No borders.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent or `#FFFFFF` background, Slate Ink text (`#0F172A`), enclosed in a 1px hairline border (`#E2E8F0`). On hover, background shifts to `#F8FAFC` with border darkening to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with Slate Ink text. Hover triggers a soft Mint wash (`#ECFDF5`) with Deep Emerald text.

### Cards
- Constructed with `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl`, styled with a `#F8FAFC` surface fill and a hairline border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`).
- Devoid of harsh internal divider lines. Content grouping relies entirely on typographic scales, column splits, and generous white space.
- Interactive cards feature a hover shift where the hairline border shifts to `#064E3B` at 20% opacity accompanied by an ambient lift.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small, highly engineered stadium pills (`rounded-full`) utilizing `label-caps` typography. Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#064E3B`. Often accompanied by an active pulse dot (`#10B981`).
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text. Hover transitions to `#E2E8F0`. Selected state transforms completely to `#064E3B` with pure white text.

### Form Inputs
- Clean fields with a solid `#F8FAFC` background, bordered by a faint `#E2E8F0` hairline. Radius set to `rounded-xl`.
- Focus state brings an instantaneous transition: background turns pure white (`#FFFFFF`), the outline sharpens to `#064E3B` (1.5px width), backed by an ethereal mint glow ring (`0 0 0 4px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes are `rounded-md` squares; radio buttons are circles. 
- Inactive: hairline border `#CBD5E1` against white.
- Checked: solid `#064E3B` fill, pure white checkmark or center pip, accompanied by a quick scale-spring micro-animation.

### Editorial & Institutional Specifics
- **Hero Statement Lockup:** Extreme contrast headline with integrated inline pill badges and hairline metric statistics.
- **Official Decrees & Agenda Rows:** Borderless list items separated by spacious `1.25rem` vertical margins, featuring hover transitions across the entire row via an ultra-light `#F8FAFC` background wash.
